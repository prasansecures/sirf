Questions

Before you book.

What do you cover for a Binance Blockchain Week side event?

Everything media for the night: photo and video crew, multi-camera recording of talks and panels with clean audio, edited photos, short clips, a recap film, and a personal brand film for the organiser. As add-ons, we offer event consultancy, teasers, speaker cards and your event's visual identity, merch people actually keep, and volunteers to run the night.

How fast do we get the content?

Everything lands within the same week: edited photos, clips, the recap and the talks, while BBW is still in everyone's feed. Given the scale of what we shoot, we'd rather deliver it properly than rush it overnight.

Can you help plan the event itself?

Yes. Event consultancy is part of what we offer: the concept, venue, run of show, guest list and partners, and a media plan, so the night works in the room and online. It can be booked on its own or with our production crew.

What is the organiser film?

A short film about you, the person behind the event: why you're building, what you're hosting and who it's for. We shoot it on the day and cut it for LinkedIn, X and Instagram, so it keeps working long after BBW.

Are you part of Binance?

No. sirf. is an independent media and content house. We're not affiliated with or endorsed by Binance or Binance Blockchain Week. We work directly with people hosting their own side events during Binance Blockchain Week in Bangkok.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the size of the event and what you need. Tell us on a short call and we'll send a clear quote the same day.

Hosting during BBW?

We're taking on a limited number of side events for Binance Blockchain Week, so each one gets a full crew. Tell us the date, the venue and the one thing you want people to remember.
