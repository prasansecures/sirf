Asked. Answered.

What is sirf.?

sirf. is a media and content house for founders and brands with high standards. We make brand films, content systems, campaigns, identity and media, and plan how it gets seen. We work with five clients at a time, so each one gets full attention.

What does “sirf” mean?

Sirf (सिर्फ़ in Hindi, صرف in Urdu) means “only”. It is how we work: one thing at a time, with full attention, so the work is excellent rather than merely finished.

How many clients does sirf. work with at once?

Five, never a sixth. Each client has one thing in focus at a time, one point of contact from start to finish, and senior people on every deliverable.

What services does sirf. offer?

Brand films, content systems for social and short-form, campaigns and launches, identity and art direction, an in-house media team for shoots, stills and edits, and strategy and distribution.

Who is sirf. for?

Founders and personal brands, consumer and D2C brands, VCs and startups, and creators and media businesses who would rather have full attention than a slice of it.

How much of my time does working with sirf. take?

About six hours a month. You get one point of contact, and you never write the same brief twice.

How do I start a project with sirf.?

Book a 30-minute call on this page, or send a short brief to sirfconvos@gmail.com. A real person replies.
