Questions

Before you book.

What do you cover for a Devcon side event?

Everything media for the night: photo and video crew, multi-camera recording of talks and panels with clean audio, edited photos, short clips, a recap film, and a personal brand film for the organiser. As add-ons, we can also make teasers, speaker cards and your event's visual identity, design merch people actually keep, and source volunteers to run the night.

How fast do we get the content?

Everything lands within the same week: edited photos, clips, the recap and the talks, while Devcon is still in everyone's feed. Given the scale of what we shoot, we'd rather deliver it properly than rush it overnight.

What is the organiser film?

A short film about you, the person behind the event: why you're building, what you're hosting and who it's for. We shoot it on the day and cut it for LinkedIn, X and Instagram, so it keeps working long after Devcon.

Are you part of Devcon?

No. sirf. is an independent media and content house. We're not affiliated with or endorsed by Devcon or the Ethereum Foundation. We work directly with people hosting their own side events during Devcon week in Mumbai.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the size of the event and what you need. Tell us on a short call and we'll send a clear quote the same day.

Hosting during Devcon?

We're taking on a limited number of side events for Devcon week, so each one gets a full crew. Tell us the date, the venue and the one thing you want people to remember.
